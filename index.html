FAQ

Before you register

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I register?

Fill in our online registration form. Our team will contact you to confirm your seat and payment details.

What documents do I need?

You upload your documents in the registration form, so you do not need to bring them on the day. Entry requirements are listed on each course.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Site Safety Supervisor and OSH Coordinator participants receive a certificate as a trained person. CIDB Green Card participants receive the CIDB Green Card.

Can my company send a group?

Yes. Contact us for group and company arrangements.

Where is the training held?

At our Training Centre in Kuantan, Pahang.
